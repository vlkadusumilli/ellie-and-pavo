Ellie & Pavo: Support

Need a hand? Write to midway_inferno_1p@icloud.com and we'll help. Please tell us your device (Apple TV, iPad or Mac) and which level you're on.

What you need to play

  • iPad: a game controller for each player (PlayStation, Xbox, Nintendo Switch Pro or other MFi controllers work).
  • Apple TV: a game controller for each player is best. The Siri Remote also plays, with the computer partner as the other hero.
  • Mac: a controller, or the keyboard (two players can share one keyboard).
  • One player can play alone, switching between Ellie and Pavo, or turn on the computer buddy in Settings.

Common questions

We're stuck. Press the hint button (LB / L1) or open the pause menu and choose Hint. Many places need both heroes: Pavo can sing away fog and hidden paths, Ellie can stomp cracked stones and carry Pavo through the wind.

A younger player finds it hard. In Settings, turn on "Extra help" for their hero: bumps and brambles don't take air, and jumps are more forgiving.

Where's my progress? It's saved automatically on this device after each level.
