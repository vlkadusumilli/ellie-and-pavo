Support

Help, controls and common questions

Need a hand? Write to midway_inferno_1p@icloud.com and we'll help. Please tell us your device (Apple TV or iPad) and which level you're on.

What you need to play

  • Apple TV and iPad: a game controller for each player (PlayStation, Xbox, Nintendo Switch Pro or other MFi controllers work).
  • Playing alone: one player can switch between Ellie and Pavo, or turn on the computer buddy in Settings.

Player 2 joins any time by pressing jump. At the end of a level, jump skips the celebration, and the special button (X / □) joins the dance.

Common questions

  • We're stuck. Press the hint button (LB / L1) or open the pause menu and choose Hint. Many places need both heroes: Pavo can sing away fog and hidden paths, Ellie can stomp cracked stones and carry Pavo through the wind.
  • A younger player finds it hard. In Settings, turn on "Extra help" for their hero: bumps and brambles don't take air, and jumps are more forgiving.
  • Where's my progress? It's saved automatically on this device after each level.
