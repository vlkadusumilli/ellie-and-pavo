A gentle co-op adventure for two. An elephant with a balloon and a peacock with a song light the lamps on the way to the festival.

Coming soon to Apple TV and iPad

Made for the family couch

Two heroes, one path

Ellie puffs, stomps and carries. Pavo sings, flutters and glides. Most places need them both.

Thirty levels, ten chapters

From the Lotus Pond to Diwali Night and the Festival, each chapter brings a new move for each hero.

Play together or alone

Two players share the screen. One player can swap between the heroes or turn on the computer buddy.

Safe and simple

No ads, no accounts, no tracking and no internet connection. The game collects no personal data.
