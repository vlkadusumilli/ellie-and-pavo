Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Ellie & Pavo, by millWorks, is a family couch co-op game for Apple TV and iPad. millWorks made it for families, and we wanted it to be simple and safe: the game collects no personal data.

What we collect

Nothing. The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no chat. It does not connect to the internet.

What the game keeps on your device

Your progress (which levels are finished, lamps, crowns and scrolls found) and your settings (music and sound volume, extra help, the computer buddy) are saved on your own device so you can carry on where you left off. They never leave the device, and we can't see them. Deleting the app deletes them.

Game controllers

The game reads your game controller only to let you play. Nothing about your controller is stored or sent anywhere.

Children

Ellie & Pavo is made for children to play with their families. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children either.

Purchases

If the game ever offers a purchase, it will be handled by Apple through the App Store, behind a grown-ups-only check. We never see payment details.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
